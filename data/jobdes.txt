Job Title: Software Engineer
Location: Remote / Hybrid / On-site
Employment Type: Full-Time

About the Role

We are seeking a motivated Software Engineer to join our growing engineering team. In this role, you will design, develop, test, and maintain scalable software solutions that power our core products. You will collaborate closely with product managers, designers, and other engineers to deliver high-quality features.

Responsibilities

Design, develop, and maintain web and backend applications

Write clean, efficient, and well-documented code

Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define and implement new features

Debug, test, and optimize application performance

Participate in code reviews and contribute to best practices

Maintain APIs and integrate third-party services

Ensure application security and data protection

Required Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field

Proficiency in one or more programming languages (Python, Java, C++, JavaScript)

Experience with web technologies (HTML, CSS, JavaScript)

Familiarity with databases (SQL or NoSQL)

Understanding of data structures, algorithms, and OOP principles

Experience with Git or other version control systems

Preferred Qualifications

Experience with frameworks such as React, Node.js, Flask, or Django

Knowledge of cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP)

Experience with RESTful APIs

Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines

Exposure to Agile or Scrum development environments

Nice to Have

Experience with Docker or containerization

Understanding of system design and scalability

Personal projects or open-source contributions

Experience building automation or data-driven applications
